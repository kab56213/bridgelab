# BridgeLab Africa — Ghana Retail Insights Challenge (Pilot)

**Challenge type:** Independent educational data analytics competition, *not* employment or an accredited internship.
**Time:** 7 days from release of the brief. Exact dates to be confirmed.
**Entry fee:** None.
**Prize:** One $100 USD award to the highest-scoring eligible submission. Specify payout channel, exchange-rate basis and transfer fees before opening entries. Tie-breaking criteria below.
**Eligibility (proposed):** Ghana-based participants aged 18+; beginner-friendly; individual entries.

## Business scenario
A fictional retailer, Akwaaba Market, operates stores in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, and Tamale. Management wants to understand sales patterns and improve performance. You receive fictional transaction data in `retail_sales_synthetic.csv`. No real customers, employees, or businesses are represented.

## Requirements
1. Inspect and clean the dataset; identify missing fields and duplicate transactions. Explain each cleaning choice.
2. Calculate sales as `quantity * unit_price_ghs * (1 - discount_pct / 100)` for valid transactions; state treatment of invalid or missing values. Do not assume missing discounts are zero without noting it.
3. Analyze sales by branch, product category, and sales channel.
4. Create at least three informative visualizations.
5. Provide three prioritized, evidence-backed business recommendations and their limitations.
6. Document methodology and assumptions so someone else can reproduce your analysis.

## Submission package
- A PDF or slide deck (up to 8 pages/slides) communicating results
- The working analysis file(s): Excel, Python notebook/script, SQL, or Power BI file
- A README with setup steps, assumptions and sources of help/tools used
- A link to a public or private repository if available (optional)

## Fairness and integrity
AI tools are allowed for assistance if disclosed; participants must understand and be able to explain their work. No plagiarism or fabrication. All entries are evaluated against the same 100-point rubric. Organizer may request a five-minute explanation of finalist work. Participant retains rights to their work. Entries will not be resold or reused for client services without separate explicit permission and appropriate terms.

## Tie break
Higher data accuracy score, then higher insight score, then independent reviewer assessment against written rubric. Organizers disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves from judging relatives/close friends where necessary.
