# Organizer Operations and Evidence Checklist

## Before recruiting
- [ ] Verify initiative name and web/domain/social conflicts
- [ ] Confirm age/eligibility and prize transfer method (net fees disclosed)
- [ ] Obtain local guidance on Ghana data protection requirements, including registration where applicable
- [ ] Create privacy notice and data retention/deletion plan
- [ ] Define published selection, rubric, ties, disputes and disqualification rules
- [ ] Arrange second impartial reviewer; avoid preferential treatment of friends
- [ ] Test challenge and document expected outputs
- [ ] Decide whether entry is open to all eligible applicants or capped; if capped, disclose selection process

## During pilot
- [ ] Issue identical dataset/instructions at the same time
- [ ] Record opt-in applications securely; minimize personal information
- [ ] Keep deadlines and FAQs available to all participants
- [ ] Allow accessibility accommodations where practical
- [ ] Record completion and rubric totals consistently

## After pilot
- [ ] Provide score breakdown and brief feedback to every participant
- [ ] Verify winner and obtain payout confirmation; do not publish proof with private details
- [ ] Publish aggregate outcomes only; ask before naming/featuring participants
- [ ] Archive your own source code, project brief, dashboard screenshots, and report
- [ ] Remove applicant contact data under the published retention schedule

## Important
Do not call participants employees or interns, promise jobs, claim corporate partnerships, or use submissions as commercial deliverables. If you later move to real business-client work, assess contracts, licensing, taxes, and employment/pay obligations first.
