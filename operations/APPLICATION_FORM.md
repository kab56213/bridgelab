# Applicant Form — Suggested Questions

Title: BridgeLab Africa — Pilot Challenge Application
Intro: Free seven-day educational analytics challenge for Ghana-based adults. One $100 USD performance prize. This is a competition, not employment, guaranteed internship placement, or accredited training.

1. Preferred name (required)
2. Email or WhatsApp contact (choose only one required communication channel)
3. Are you 18 or older? (yes/no; do not request date of birth or ID)
4. Are you based in Ghana? (yes/no)
5. Which tool would you use? (Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, Other)
6. How would you describe your analytics experience? (beginner/intermediate/advanced)
7. What would you like to learn? (short response; optional)
8. Can you complete a seven-day independent challenge? (yes/no)
9. Agree to published competition rules and privacy notice? (yes/no)

Before launch, write a privacy notice specifying the organizer/contact, why data is collected, access, storage/retention, deletion requests, and whether data crosses borders. Confirm whether Ghana DPC registration and other requirements apply before collecting applications.
